About the Platform

The KSP Crime Analytics AI Platform is designed to assist Karnataka State Police with crime analysis, AI-powered conversations, predictive analytics, hotspot detection, and intelligent reporting. The system integrates modern Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Flask APIs, and a secure database to improve crime investigation and decision-making.
